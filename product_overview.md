# Tallowbrook — product overview (fictional)

Tallowbrook is a B2B web and mobile app that small field-service businesses
(HVAC, plumbing, electrical) use to schedule and track jobs.

## Roles
- **Owner** — full account access, billing, integrations
- **Dispatcher** — schedules and assigns jobs, manages customers
- **Technician** — mobile app; views and updates assigned jobs
- **Customer** — limited portal: upcoming appointments, invoices

## Platforms
- Web app for office staff
- iOS and Android apps for technicians

## Data
Customer names, addresses, and phone numbers; job notes and photos;
invoices; payment status.

## Integrations
- Stripe (payments)
- Twilio (SMS)
- Google Calendar
- QuickBooks Online
